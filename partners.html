Development Finance Partners

DFI co-investment
partners & co-lenders

The Fund structures each investment to attract DFI co-financing at the senior debt level. These institutions have active programmes in TAP I target markets.

World Bank Group
IFC
International Finance Corporation — the world's largest multilateral development finance institution focused on the private sector. Active across all TAP I primary markets with long-tenor debt facilities.
African Development Bank Group
AfDB
The African Development Bank Group provides debt, equity, and guarantees for private sector projects across Africa. Its Sustainable Energy Fund for Africa (SEFA) provides technical assistance grants.
Agence Française de Développement
Proparco
France's private sector development finance institution. Active in East Africa, Madagascar, and Indian Ocean markets. Has committed over €200M to Rwandan projects alone.
German Development Finance
DEG
Deutsche Investitions- und Entwicklungsgesellschaft — Germany's DFI providing long-term finance for private enterprises in developing countries. Active Namibia and Southern Africa mandates.
Netherlands Development Finance
FMO
Dutch entrepreneurial development bank. Focuses on renewable energy, food & agri, and financial institutions in East Africa. Active Rwanda programme with over €150M committed.
Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency
MIGA
World Bank Group political risk insurance arm. Covers expropriation, currency transfer restrictions, and breach of contract by host governments across all TAP I target markets.
Governance Standards

Built to the
standards that matter

IFC Performance Standards
Environmental and social risk management framework used as the global benchmark for private sector development finance.
EU Green Taxonomy
European Union's classification system for sustainable economic activities. Required for SFDR Article 8/9 funds investing alongside EU capital.
OECD Blended Finance Principles
Five principles for mobilising commercial finance for sustainable development in developing economies. TAP I is structured to comply with all five.
TCFD
Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures. The Fund reports climate risks and opportunities in line with TCFD recommendations across all portfolio investments.
