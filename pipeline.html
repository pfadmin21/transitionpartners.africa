Investment Criteria

What the Fund
looks for.

TAP I deploys into construction-completion and early-operational stage climate infrastructure. Every qualifying project must meet mandatory criteria before preferred attributes are assessed.

Mandatory Criteria · All must be met
  • Located in a TAP I target jurisdiction with an active Mauritius DTAA
  • Climate-positive or climate-resilient asset — renewable energy, clean water, coastal resilience, or nature-based solutions
  • Contracted or quasi-contracted revenue stream — PPA, water offtake, or FiT with sovereign or investment-grade counterparty
  • Equity ticket between USD 3M and USD 15M per project
  • DFI co-financing available at the senior debt level (IFC, AfDB, Proparco, DEG, FMO, or equivalent)
  • Project sponsor with demonstrated track record in the relevant sector and jurisdiction
  • Willingness to structure under a Mauritius GBC SPV
Preferred Attributes · Weighted in scoring
  • USD or EUR-denominated offtake — reduces currency risk for LP investors
  • MIGA or ATI political risk insurance available or in process
  • Carbon credit revenue potential under VCS or REDD+ frameworks
  • Technical Assistance grant eligibility (GCF, SEFA, bilateral donors)
  • Gender-lens or SDG 5 components embedded in project design
  • Municipal, sovereign, or multilateral off-taker (not single commercial counterparty)
  • Project at construction-completion stage — reduces construction risk significantly
Exclusion List · Absolute
  • Fossil fuel production, extraction, or refining
  • Coal-fired power generation or coal-related infrastructure
  • Projects with pending IFC PS violations or unresolved community objections
  • Jurisdictions under FATF grey or black list at time of investment
  • Weapons, tobacco, gambling, or adult content
  • Projects with significant involuntary resettlement without IFC PS-compliant RAP
