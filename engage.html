What is the minimum investment ticket? +
The Fund targets equity tickets of USD 3–15M per project, with DFI debt co-financing typically bringing total project values to USD 15–80M. LP commitments to the Fund itself start at USD 5M.
Is the Fund open to non-DFI investors? +
Yes. While DFI co-financing is required at the project level, LP investors in the Fund include pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, family offices, and ESG-mandated allocators. The Fund is structured to satisfy IFRS 10 B11 requirements for European institutional investors.
Why is the Fund domiciled in Mauritius? +
Mauritius provides 45 Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements covering all target markets, FSC regulatory oversight that European institutional investors recognise, and a GBC structure that allows treaty-protected capital flows. It eliminates 10–30% withholding tax that would otherwise erode LP economics.
How do I submit a project for review? +
Use the Project Submission pathway on our Private Access page. You'll be guided through a short eligibility check, project details form, and document checklist. The investment team reviews all submissions within 10 business days.
What sectors does the Fund invest in? +
Renewable energy (solar PV, wind, hydro), distributed and off-grid infrastructure, clean water treatment and distribution, coastal resilience, and nature-based solutions including blue carbon and REDD+. Fossil fuel projects are excluded from the mandate.
Is this a public offering? +
No. TAP I is a Professional CIS under FSC Mauritius regulation. It is not publicly offered and is accessible only through introduction. All communications are treated as strictly confidential.
